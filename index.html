How it works

Three steps to get it back

1

Finder uploads it

Whoever finds an item posts a photo, where it was found, and where it's being kept.

2

Owner submits proof

Spot your item? Describe something only the owner would know: what's inside, a scratch, the lock screen.

3

Approved & collected

Security checks the proof and gives you a 6-digit pickup code. Show it with your student ID to collect.
